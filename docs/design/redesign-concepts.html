Stoke · design directions · 3 concepts

Make it feel alive

Three ways the app could look and feel, each shown on the screens you use most: Today, logging food, and a live workout. The numbers are a real fat-loss day from the new goal-based targets (2,330 kcal, 185 g protein), with UAE food from the catalogue. Pick one, or mix: tell me which letter, and what you'd steal from the others.

A · Ember B · Glass C · Mission control Ideas list
A

Ember

Built around the Stoke name and the flame icon. Warm dark, bold condensed numbers like a scoreboard, and one fire gradient that marks anything you can act on. Calories read as fuel left in the tank. The fastest-feeling of the three, and the most distinctive on a home screen.

  • Fuel-gauge calories
  • One-tap smart nudges
  • Raised "+" log button
  • Bottom-sheet logging
  • Giant set-logging dials
  • Live plate picture
  • PR celebration
B

Glass

Feels native to iOS 26: frosted, translucent cards over a soft colour wash, Apple-style concentric rings, and a floating dock. Calm, light and premium, with the coach front and centre. The closest to Apple Fitness.

  • Liquid-glass cards
  • Concentric rings
  • Floating dock
  • Coach as a chat with action cards
  • Light first, dark too
C

Mission control

For data lovers: an instrument panel on a fine grid, monospaced numbers, segmented bars that light up, a training heat-map and a live event feed. Dense and very "tech", like Whoop or a race-car dashboard. Best for Progress; heavy for everyday logging.

  • Segmented LED bars
  • Weekly heat-map
  • Live event feed
  • Mono numerals
  • Neon status colours
+

Ideas that work with any look

These change how the app feels in the hand. Most fit whichever concept you choose.

Logging

Log from anywhere in two taps

A raised "+" opens a sheet over the current screen: search, scan, photo, voice, menu. No page change, so it feels instant.

Logging

Habits become one-tap nudges

"Karak at 4 pm again?", "Same breakfast as yesterday?" Built from your own history, never a guess.

Logging

Swipe actions

Swipe a food left to delete, right to duplicate, long-press to move it to another meal.

Logging

Amount slider with haptic ticks

Drag between ½, 1, 1½ servings and feel each step; calories left update live before you confirm.

Training

One set at a time

A focus mode with huge weight and rep dials, the plates drawn on the bar, and rest on the lock screen and watch.

Training

Record celebrations

A short burst, a strong haptic and a shareable card when you beat an estimated 1-rep max or a rep record.

Motion

Numbers that count

Calories and macros roll to their new value after logging; rings sweep in on open. Turned off when "reduce motion" is on.

Motion

Shared-element transitions

Tap a food and its card grows into the detail sheet instead of a page sliding in.

Motivation

Streak flame

The Stoke flame grows with consecutive fully logged days and dims (never shames) when you miss one.

Progress

Weekly story

Every Monday a swipeable recap: trend weight, best lift, most-eaten food, adherence. Shareable.

Time of day

A home that changes through the day

Morning shows sleep and weigh-in, midday shows food, evening shows what's left and tomorrow's workout.

Polish

Shimmer loading and empty states with art

Skeletons that shimmer, and illustrated empty states that tell you the one thing to do next.

My recommendation

Ember as the base, with the best of B and C

Ember gives Stoke its own identity and matches the flame icon. I'd borrow B's glass sheets for logging and the coach chat, and C's dashboard panels for the Progress tab, where data density helps. Then build in this order: Today and the log sheet, the live workout, motion and celebrations, the weekly story.

Mockups use example data from a fat-loss day. Fonts and colours are proposals; everything here is built as real screens in the app after you pick a direction.
