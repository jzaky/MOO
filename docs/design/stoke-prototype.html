Under the hood

  • The command bar uses the app's existing text parser first (instant, offline) and Claude only for what it can't read.
  • Plate scan uses the existing recognition pipeline: separate stages, gram ranges, confidence tiers, confirm before logging.
  • Every number is calculated by code from the food database; the AI never does the arithmetic.
  • Dynamic Island, Live Activity and widgets are the iOS pieces already written; this is their design.
  • Motion respects "Reduce motion"; haptics on set done, record and slider ticks.

What makes it feel more tech

Input, not navigationSay or type what happened; the app figures out where it goes.
Show certaintyRanges and confidence everywhere the data is estimated, exact numbers only where they are.
Live surfacesIsland, lock screen, widgets and watch carry the moment so you rarely open the app.
Instrument detailMono numerals, segment meters, scan lines: precise, not decorative.
Physical feedbackHaptics and motion confirm every action; a record feels like an event.
Proactive, never pushySuggestions come from your own habits and can always be ignored.
